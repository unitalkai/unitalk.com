## 1. Product

Unitalk lets anyone create and own a **Public AI Collaborator**.

A Public AI Collaborator is a persistent AI identity that can represent a person or professional online, communicate with people, remember context, use tools, and perform work.

It is not a chatbot profile. It is not an AI assistant. It is not a prompt. It is an **AI Collaborator with a public presence**.

### Core promise
> **Meet your AI Collaborator.** Create yours from a URL.

### Brand promise
> **Own your intelligence.**

## 2. Product thesis

The Web gave everyone a website. Social networks gave everyone a profile. AI can give everyone a **Collaborator**.

The important shift is not better chat. It is giving a person a persistent AI identity that can: speak, remember, know, create, connect, work.

The product must make this concept tangible rather than explain it academically.

## 3. The first Collaborator

The primary public demonstration is **Patrick Chassany's AI Collaborator**. Patrick Chassany is the founder of Unitalk. The Collaborator represents Patrick's public professional knowledge and can talk about: Patrick, his work, his companies, his ideas, Unitalk.

It must be a real, usable Collaborator. It must not be presented as a fictional mascot.

Do not call it Pacha. Pacha is a future separate Collaborator and is not the first public demo.

## 4. The homepage

The homepage is not a conventional SaaS landing page. The homepage is the **public profile of Patrick's AI Collaborator**. The visitor should experience the product before reading about it.

Primary sequence: **MEET → TALK → WANT ONE → CREATE**

Do not interrupt this sequence with feature explanations.

## 5. Homepage content

### Hero
**Meet Patrick's AI Collaborator** / Patrick Chassany / Founder of Unitalk / ● Online

The Collaborator introduces itself:
> Hi. I'm Patrick's AI Collaborator. Ask me anything about Patrick, his work, or Unitalk. Or give me something to do.

Primary CTA: **Talk to me →**

## 6. The "Want one?" moment

### WANT ONE?
Create your AI Collaborator in one click.

Input: **Start with a URL.** Placeholder `yourwebsite.com`. Accepts domain, website URL, public social profile URL (personal website, company website, LinkedIn profile, public social profile).

Do not force a long form. Do not ask for a biography. Do not ask for a prompt. The URL is the starting point.

CTA: **Create my Collaborator →**
Supporting copy: **7 days free or 5M tokens. No credit card.**

## 7. Creation flow

> **One URL → One AI Collaborator**

The system uses the supplied public URL to discover and structure available public information. May collect: public profile information, website content, public professional information, public social content where accessible, public articles, public descriptions, public expertise, public products/services, public links.

Principle: **Create first. Configure later.**

## 8. The Collaborator

Persistent components:
- **Identity**: name, role, avatar, public URL, email where enabled, phone where enabled, voice
- **Soul**: personality, communication style, rules, values, boundaries
- **Knowledge**: public information, private information, documents, websites, FAQs, professional knowledge
- **Memory**: persistent information learned through interaction and work. Knowledge = what it knows. Memory = what it remembers.
- **Skills**: research, sales, writing, analysis, coding, lead qualification
- **Tools**: web, search, email, calendar, CRM, phone, messaging, APIs, applications
- **Work**: the actual missions, tasks, results and history

## 9. Public means public

People can discover it, open its profile, read about it, talk to it, give it appropriate work, understand what it represents. Not simply an account dashboard. Its public profile is part of the product.

**The profile is alive.** It should feel more like meeting someone than visiting a settings page.

## 10. Voice

Voice is a first-class product capability. The visitor should be able to start a conversation, interrupt where supported, ask follow-up questions, give a task, continue a previous conversation. The same Collaborator identity must persist across supported channels: web, voice, phone, email, messaging. Do not present channels as disconnected products.

## 11. Work

> **A chatbot answers. A Collaborator works.**

A Collaborator must receive a goal and perform work using its knowledge, skills and tools. Example: "Find 20 potential Unitalk resellers in France."

Show meaningful progress: Working… Researching. Qualifying. Preparing. Then: **20 found. 7 qualified. 3 high potential.**

Do not fabricate results. Demo data must be clearly real or clearly simulated.

## 12. Ownership

The user owns the Collaborator's identity, soul, knowledge, memory, skills, tools, permissions, configuration, work history. The underlying model or runtime can change. The Collaborator remains.

> **The brain can change. The Collaborator remains yours.**

## 13. Pricing and trial

- **Collaborator — €9 / month**: one AI Collaborator.
- **Workforce — From €49 / month**: multiple Collaborators working together.
- First experience: **7 days free OR 5M tokens**. No credit card required.

The user should not encounter payment before experiencing the product.

## 14. Workforce

Multiple Collaborators working together. Start with one, add more when needed. Examples: Sales, Research, Marketing, Operations, Development. Do not introduce Workforce heavily on the first screen. The homepage sells the first Collaborator.

## 15. Conversion model

Primary conversion: **Visitor → Collaborator owner**. Not Visitor → account, dashboard, or subscription.

North-star loop: **DISCOVER → TALK → CREATE → WORK → OWN**
Long-term: **CREATE → OWN → WORK → EARN → CREATE**

## 16. Product language

Always prefer **AI Collaborator**. Use **AI worker** only when explaining the concept in plain language.
Avoid: AI agent, chatbot, assistant, copilot, bot, AI employee, digital twin.
Do not call the Collaborator a "profile". A profile is static. A Collaborator works.

## 17. Copy principles

Be direct. Short sentences. Simple words. Concrete claims.
Show, don't explain. One idea per section. Never create a section just because a feature exists.
No SaaS language: platform, ecosystem, seamless, next-generation, revolutionary, powerful, intelligent automation, all-in-one, end-to-end, unlock, transform your workflow.
Never oversell.

## 18. What the homepage must NOT become

Not: a feature encyclopedia, a generic AI landing page, a chatbot comparison page, a pricing-first SaaS page, a technical architecture page, an enterprise sales page, an AI agent marketplace, a giant integration grid, a long manifesto.

The homepage has one job: **Make someone want their own Public AI Collaborator.**

## 19. Product success test

A new visitor should understand within seconds: (1) This is an AI Collaborator. (2) Patrick has one. (3) I can talk to it. (4) I can have one too. (5) I can create it from my URL. (6) I can try it without a credit card.

## 20. The ultimate test

Do not ask "Does this explain Unitalk?" Ask **"Does this make me want one?"**
