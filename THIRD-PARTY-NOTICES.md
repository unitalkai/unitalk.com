# Open Intelligent UI

The full gallery, component library, functions, demo data, media services and styles are also used as the `open-intelligent-ui` source dependency, pinned to commit `03213439358bf3408ad61a46ec9e3230c5572e48`. The eight starter labels/prompts in `src/lib/public-intelligent-starters.ts` come from its `src/app/page.tsx`. The seven additional starter programs in this app are locally authored. The dependency includes the upstream MIT license reproduced below; its third-party dependencies retain their own licenses.

The scoped OpenUI library and rendering pattern in `src/components/public-openui-response.tsx` and bootstrap opt-out in `src/lib/public-openui-devtools.ts` are adapted from [thesysdev/open-intelligent-ui](https://github.com/thesysdev/open-intelligent-ui), especially `src/lib/components/{layout,text,controls}.tsx`, `src/app/gallery/gallery.tsx` and `src/lib/devtools.ts`, retrieved 2026-10-21. The layout is adapted to Unitalk; dependencies retain their own licenses.
