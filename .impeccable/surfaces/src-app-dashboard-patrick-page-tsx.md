# Patrick's dashboard

Mode: Operate. Owner reviews decisions, inspects demo work and relationships, and understands the Collaborator's knowledge and permissions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The owner sees what needs a person first. Keep the existing You/Me/People/Collaborator sections inside this surface, separate from global destinations.

OWN-WORLD: WhatsApp cream and lightweight sans-serif; fuchsia selections, understated hairlines and rounded standard controls. Dense enough to operate, without marketing-sized dashboard titles.

STORY: A persistent demo notice states the role and simulated data. Choose a decision, read its draft inline, approve locally or leave it pending. No external messages are sent.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Role-aware navigation, owner identity strip, four section buttons, concise greeting, pending decision list beside an inline reading panel. A confirmation reports the local outcome.

FORM: User-pinned reference; standard dashboard layout and controls, code-led. Mobile stacks decisions and their details without horizontal overflow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
